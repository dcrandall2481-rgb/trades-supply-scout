# Starting US supplier set

Use this list when building comparisons. Verify robots.txt, public pricing, and Pro-login walls before treating a site as automated.

## National / multi-region

| Supplier | Strength | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| Home Depot | Broad stock, store pickup, Pro Xtra | Online price may differ from store; Pro price often behind login |
| Lowe's | Broad stock, store pickup, Pro program | Same login-wall issue on contractor price |
| Ferguson / Ferguson Home / Build.com | Plumbing, HVAC, fixtures, PVF | Trade price often after account; branch will-call vs ship |
| Grainger | MRO, fasteners, PPE, electrical commodities | Industrial pack sizes; list vs contract price |
| SupplyHouse | Plumbing and HVAC catalog depth | Shipping on bulky items changes landed cost |
| Fastenal | Fasteners, abrasives, shop supplies | Branch inventory vs web |
| Ace | Local pickup for small fills | Pricing and stock are store-dependent |

## Trade-specialist add-ons

| Trade | Add when relevant |
|---|---|

## Do not treat as primary

- Auction or classified sites
- Social marketplaces
- Non-US storefronts
- Any firearms or firearm-parts retailer

## Before adding a supplier

1. Confirm it is a US company selling to trades or public.
2. Read robots.txt and terms on automated access.
3. Note whether public price is list, map, or login-gated.
4. Record how pack units appear so comparisons stay honest.
